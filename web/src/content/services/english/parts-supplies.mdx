We offer a wide range of spare parts for all the machines in our offer. We simplify the process of identifying and ordering the correct part, offering advice to make the purchase as simple as possible.

* **Wear parts**: blades (rotor and stator), teeth and knives are frequently replaced due to constant wear.
* **Separation and screening components**: screens, grids and perforation plates for separating materials by size.
* **Mechanical and electrical assemblies**: motors, gearboxes and drive components.
* **Hydraulic and pneumatic systems**: hoses, seals, pumps and cylinders.
* **Conveyor and material handling parts**: belts, pulleys and augers.

## Black soft wire

Black soft wire is, due to the hardening process, perfect for all types of waste balers. Being soft, lubricated, having the same diameter along its entire length, it is the perfect option from an economic point of view. The rollers are precisely made ensuring perfect unwinding during daily use. If the wire does not tangle then it does not break either. This type of wire guarantees minimal wear on the binding system, extending the life and efficiency of the press. From our warehouse in Brașov we can deliver quickly anywhere in the country.

### Technical specifications

* **Diameter:** 3.2 mm
* **Tolerance:** 0.02 / + 0.08 mm
* **Tear resistance:** +/- 2 kg/mm2
